What is Fostering?

Fostering means giving a rescued animal a temporary home while NGOs and shelters work to find them a permanent family. You provide food, shelter, and love — and when a permanent home is found, the animal moves on. It's a beautiful way to help without the full commitment of adoption. NGOs often have limited space, so foster parents are critical.
